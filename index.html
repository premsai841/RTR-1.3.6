QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

What is RTR Charts?

RTR Charts is a TradingView indicator designed to organize directional signals, trade levels and multi-timeframe market context on one chart.

Is RTR a trading bot?

No. RTR is an indicator. It does not place trades automatically and should not be treated as a guarantee of trading outcomes.

Does RTR work for forex?

RTR is designed with forex traders in mind and can be applied to supported TradingView markets. Always test settings on the specific market and timeframe you trade.

Do I need TradingView?

Yes. RTR is intended to be used inside TradingView. The exact access and installation method should be confirmed at purchase.

Can I share my access?

Access terms depend on the plan and delivery method. Keep your credentials, invite links and purchased materials private.
